## Binding and direct-FFI review

`Cargo.toml` requires `ffmpeg-next = "9"`; the lock resolves `ffmpeg-next` and
`ffmpeg-sys-next` 9.0.0. The candidate `Cargo.lock` SHA-256 is
`92795262d7ee3900944b82c0ce6381253adbb2c86fa4d0aa876aebc26bf3daba`.
`ffmpeg-sys-next` 9 requires `cc >=1.3`, so `cc 1.4.4` and its exact
`find-msvc-tools 0.1.11` dependency are the only resolver collateral beyond the
binding/package version changes; an unrelated `windows-sys` consolidation was
removed before validation.

The FFmpeg 9 generated bindings and headers were reviewed at every direct-FFI
boundary, then compiled and exercised:

- `src/video/codec_motion.rs` and `src/codec_mosh.rs`: `AVMotionVector` size,
  unaligned side-data records, bounds, source/reference semantics, and
  `AVCodecContext` corrupt/export flags;
- `src/video/decoder.rs`: export-motion flags, `AV_NOPTS_VALUE`, time bases,
  `avformat_seek_file`, seek/flush, reordered-frame EOF drain, selected-frame
  identity, loop generation, and software codec opening;
- `src/video/source_descriptor.rs`: `AVCodecParameters`, frame crop fields,
  display-matrix side data, pixel-format flags, field order, swscale coefficient
  selection, and `sws_setColorspaceDetails`;
- `src/video/hw_decode.rs`: D3D11VA pixel format, hardware context ownership,
  `av_hwframe_transfer_data`, and frame-property transfer. This evaluator stays
  disconnected from production decode and makes no new capability claim;
- `src/audio/clip.rs`: decoder EOF drain and libswresample flush behavior.

No direct interrupt-callback hook is present in these paths. Existing bounded
packet scans, allocation ledgers, queue limits, seek limits, generation checks,
and worker-retirement laws remain unchanged.
