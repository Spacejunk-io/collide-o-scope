# R2 at-most-two Mosh-domain planner research

The data-only planner in `src/mosh_domains.rs` admits zero, one, or two stable
domain identities and no more. Deletion leaves a permanent bounded tombstone;
each live domain owns a distinct mutable-history identity, latest-only mailbox,
recipe digest, raster/job/wake/retained-frame charge, and worker slot.

The artistic order is frozen as: admitted layer/group composition, domain tap
and premultiplied influence return, then the one shared final temporal/VHS
finish and absolute blackout. This is a proposed new order only. The existing
single final-program Codec-Mosh remains the exact old-patch path.

The 720p prototype caps each edge at 1280, each raster at 921,600 pixels, each
job at 8 MiB, each wake lattice at 4,096 cells, each history at four retained
frames, workers at two, and the aggregate physical plan at 48 MiB. Tests prove
zero-domain zero resources, no speculative second-domain allocation, fixed
physical bytes across 1 versus 100,000 sends, one-byte-under refusal, permanent
tombstones, distinct history owners, and depth-one latest-only mailboxes.

No codec worker, GPU path, patch schema, panel, Morph/modulation/Dice surface,
export sidecar, or artistic promotion is included. The required two-domain
720p30 p95/p99 and named recording review have not been run, so the candidate
remains research-only and cannot be presented as an available capability.
